1. Digital product license

Unless a product page says otherwise, every purchase includes a personal, non-transferable license to use the files for yourself. You can read them on any device you own and print a copy for personal use.

What you may not do

  • Resell, sublicense, share or give away the files unless the product page explicitly includes resale or MRR rights.
  • Upload the files to a free download site, membership, torrent or file-sharing service.
  • Claim authorship or copyright of the material.

Where a product includes resale rights (MRR), the exact scope is stated on that product's Payhip page. If it is not stated there, resale rights are not included.

2. Delivery & access

Files are delivered by Payhip immediately after a successful payment. Your download link is emailed to the address you use at checkout. Check your spam or promotions folder if it does not arrive within a few minutes.

If your link expires, you can request a fresh one from Payhip or contact us through the store. Access is not a subscription — there is nothing to cancel.

3. Refunds & defective files

Digital products are delivered instantly and cannot be returned, so all sales are final once the download link has been issued. This does not affect your statutory rights.

If a file is corrupted, missing, or will not open, contact us through the Payhip store with your order email and a short description. We will replace the file or fix access. If we cannot fix it, we will make it right.

EU/UK buyers: where consumer law gives you a withdrawal right, you expressly agree that delivery begins immediately and acknowledge that you lose the withdrawal right once the download has started.

4. Payments, currency & taxes

Payments are processed securely by Payhip. We never see or store your card details. PayPal and major debit/credit cards are supported; available methods are shown at checkout.

Prices are listed in US dollars. Payhip shows the final amount in your local currency at checkout, including any VAT or sales tax that applies to your country.

EU/UK VAT is handled by Payhip as merchant of record. Your receipt is emailed by Payhip after purchase.

5. Privacy & cookies

This website does not create accounts and does not ask for payment details. If you request the free hooks, your email is submitted through Netlify Forms so we can send the resource and occasional shop updates. You can unsubscribe at any time.

Purchases are handled by Payhip. Payhip processes your name, email, payment and tax information under its own privacy policy. See the Payhip privacy policy for details.
